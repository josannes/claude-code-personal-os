# Money

Applies to everything under `03_money/`. Inherits the root `CLAUDE.md`.

## Structure

- `overview.md`: income, fixed costs, what is left each month, and where balances are
  checked (not the balances)
- `subscriptions.md`: recurring payments, including AI tools, with price and renewal date
- `budget.md`: the plan, if you keep one

When `overview.md` exists, it is the source other folders check when something costs money
(a project, a tool, a trip).

## Rules

**Numbers have a date and a source.** A number without a date is worthless after three
months. Account balances are not written down at all: write where they are checked.

**No account numbers, card numbers or logins** in any file, ever. If one comes up, say so
and keep it out of the brain.

**Calculations, not investment advice.** Claude can do the maths on a budget, a loan or a
savings plan, but does not recommend what to invest in.
