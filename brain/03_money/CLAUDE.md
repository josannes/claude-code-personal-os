# Money

Applies to everything under `03_money/`. Inherits the root `CLAUDE.md`.

## Structure

- `overview.md`: income, fixed costs, and where balances are checked (not the balances)
- `budget.md`: the plan, if you keep one

## Rules

**No volatile numbers.** Account balances go stale the day they are written. Write where
the number is checked. Fixed amounts (rent, subscriptions) are fine, with a date.

**No account numbers, card numbers or logins** in any file, ever.
