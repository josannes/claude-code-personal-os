# Work

Applies to everything under `02_work/`. Inherits the root `CLAUDE.md`.

## Structure

- `current/<job>/`: the job you have now, with its own `CLAUDE.md` and `status.md`
- `applications/<company>-<role>-<YYYY-MM>/`: one folder per application, made with `/new-project`
- `cv/`: your CV source and versions

## Rules

**Read before writing.** A new application starts by reading `00_me/PROFILE.md`, the CV and
earlier applications. If you find yourself explaining who you are, the system has failed,
and that is worth fixing in `PROFILE.md`.

**Never oversell.** Skills and experience come from `00_me/PROFILE.md`, at the level written
there. Claude does not upgrade them in an application without asking.
